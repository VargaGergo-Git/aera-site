Heart rate variability

Heart rate variability is the tiny change in time from one heartbeat to the next, made by the nerves that steer your heart. A healthy heart at rest keeps varying, low variation has gone with early death in heart patients, and your own usual is the fair comparison.

4

Is a higher number better?

After a heart attack, low variation has gone with early death. Among healthy adults, normal covers a huge range.

Questions

Before you start.

What does Aera do?

It reads what your watch writes to Apple Health and says, in plain words, how your sleep, stress, body age and runs sit against your own usual.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Aera reads Apple Health, so a Garmin, Polar or COROS watch works too. Without one you still get movement, workouts and any sleep your iPhone records; the heart reads lean on a watch.

Is Aera free?

Yes, to start. Your daily readings with their last 30 days, routes, and Flyovers of the last 7 days are free. Premium keeps your whole history and every Flyover.

Do I need an account, and where does my data go?

There is no account. Aera works out every reading on your iPhone, and your health numbers stay there. The privacy policy lists what does leave, such as map lookups.

How does Aera know what is normal for me?

It learns your usual from your own nights and sessions over the first weeks. Until it has enough, it says it is still learning instead of guessing.
