What Aera is

Aera is an iPhone and Apple Watch health app made by one developer in Hungary. It reads Apple Health and reads your sleep, heart, training and progress against your own usual, and every number opens a short note on how it was worked out. Runs recorded on an Apple Watch or iPhone can be replayed in 3D over the real ground, with pace and heart rate. Everything is computed on the phone. There is no account.

What stays free

Today's reads and their last 30 days, the 3D replay of any run from the past 7 days, and the last 30 days of every export. You can try all of it before deciding.

What Lifetime opens

Your full history past 30 days: Aera's scores, the sleep score, trends, sleep debt, every export at full length and the PDFs for your doctor. The 3D replay of any older run. More than five saved routes.

How to get the offer

Install Aera, open Settings, then Premium, tap All plans and pick Lifetime between 27 November and 1 December 2026. The discounted price is set by Apple in every App Store. Questions: hello@aerahealth.app.
