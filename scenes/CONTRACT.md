# Scene contract (for scenes/<name>/)

Each scene is a self-contained cinematic scroll section for the Aera homepage
(index.html EN, hu.html HU, de.html DE are generated by scripts/build_home.py;
the lead integrates your scene, you never edit build_home.py, home.css, home.js
or the generated pages).

Files you write, all in scenes/<name>/:
- section.html : ONE <section class="scene scene-<name>" id="<name>"> ... </section>
  fragment. Text uses placeholders {{key}} looked up in strings.json.
  Any SVG ids must be prefixed "<name>-" (page has other SVGs).
- strings.json : {"en": {...}, "hu": {...}, "de": {...}} same keys in all three.
  Native-sounding Hungarian and German, not literal. Plain words. No em dashes
  or en dashes, never the word "AI", no emoji, no readiness score numbers.
- scene.css : all rules scoped under .scene-<name>. Use home.css tokens:
  var(--bg) var(--bg-2) var(--card) var(--ink) var(--ink-2) var(--ink-3)
  var(--line) var(--green) var(--indigo) var(--gold) var(--pink) var(--orange)
  var(--sage) var(--shadow) var(--radius) var(--ease) var(--bounce)
  var(--rounded) (font). Reuse page classes: .wide (centered container with
  gutter), .kicker, .h2, .sub, .split (headline split into words by home.js),
  .reveal (fades in when scrolled to; needs nothing from you).
  Light and dark: tokens switch by @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark); any
  literal colour you add needs a dark counterpart in that media query.
- scene.js : optional, and only for things CSS cannot do (a video that plays
  when in view, say). Never read the scroll position, never pin, never write
  styles per frame. One IIFE, no globals, try/catch, static state on error.

Behaviour rules (stability rebuild, 2026-10-09, after the Apple and Bevel study):
- No pins and no scroll-driven motion. The page scrolls natively. A scene is a
  finished still composition (real screens in device frames, drawn maps) that
  fades up once with .reveal. A film is a short pre-rendered video with a
  poster image, played once when in view, never scrubbed by scroll.
- Words sit beside or below a device, never drawn over it.
- Phones first: must look great and fit at 320, 390 px wide (no element wider
  than the viewport, no horizontal scroll) and at 1440x900 desktop.
- Light and dark both gorgeous.
- Fast: keep markup modest (target < 25 KB per scene incl. inline SVG).
- Images available (real app captures, 1206x2622, 400w and 800w webp):
  ../../img/<name>-400.webp / -800.webp where name in: home-light, home-dark,
  sleep-light, sleep-dark, recovery-light, recovery-dark, session-light,
  session-dark, planner-light, planner-dark, article-sleep-light,
  article-sleep-dark, article-recovery-dark, routenav, workout-detail,
  workout-flyover-dark. In section.html write paths as img/... (page root);
  your test page sits two folders down so it must rewrite or use a <base>.
  A phone frame exists in home.css: <div class="phone"><div class="screen-wrap"><img ...></div></div>.
- Claims: only what your brief lists. Illustrations (drawn watch, drawn widgets,
  drawn maps) must be clearly stylised, not fake screenshots.

Test: write scenes/<name>/test.html that loads ../../home.css, your
scene.css, your section (EN strings substituted), ../../home.js and your
scene.js, with <base href="../../"> so img/ paths work; render with Playwright
(import { chromium } from '/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs';
chromium.launch({executablePath:'/opt/pw-browsers/chromium'})) at 390x844 dpr2
and 1440x900, light and dark, at several scroll positions through the pin,
plus with reducedMotion:'reduce'. Look at the images and iterate until it is
genuinely beautiful (think Apple product pages, Flighty, Bevel). Check:
no page errors, documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth === 0 at 320/390/1440.
Screenshots and scripts go in /tmp/claude-0/scenes/<name>/. Do NOT commit or
push; do not switch git branches.

Final message under 200 words: files, what the scene does frame by frame,
screenshot paths, check results, any claim you were unsure of.
